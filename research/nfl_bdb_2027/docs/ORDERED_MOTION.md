## Measurement and equations

Retain the previously fixed WR Combine drill mapping and first qualifying heading-change anchor. For each of 495 attempts from 106 receivers, take windows with radius 0.6, 1.0 and 1.4 seconds. Endpoints clip to the observed trace. Smooth positions with the existing seven-frame Savitzky–Golay filter. Construct channels of entry-normalized speed, acceleration divided by 5, absolute heading rate, and cumulative entry-normalized distance. These derived channels inherit position smoothing and 10 Hz resolution; an angular derivative near low positional speed can be unstable. They do not measure forces directly.

For each window calculate four means, four standard deviations, four endpoint increments and six signed channel areas:

\[
A_{ij}=\tfrac12\sum_t \{\bar z_{t,i}\Delta z_{t,j}-\bar z_{t,j}\Delta z_{t,i}\}.
\]

This discrete geometric observation distinguishes order; reversing the sample order reverses its sign. Areas have the chosen channel scales' units. They are not invariant under arbitrary scaling or time reparameterization and should not be interpreted as literal physical work.

The phase controller has states approach, brake, redirect, recover. A frame stays in its current state or advances one state. Paths start at approach and finish at recover; incomplete hypothetical phases are not an extra accepted terminal option. With normalized acceleration a and absolute heading rate w, use fixed costs one half of `(a²+w²)`, `((a+1)²+w²)`, `(a²+(w−1)²)` and `((a−1)²+w²)`. These fixed templates are hypotheses. Define a path weight as the exponential of minus total cost. Forward/backward log-sum recurrences compute its partition mass and marginal state occupations without enumerating all paths. Record four expected occupancy fractions, four phase-conditioned mean accelerations and log partition mass divided by frame count. Log mass depends on the number of admissible segmentations as well as their costs. Thus each window has 27 descriptors, or 81 across three candidate windows.

Independent enumeration of every accepting six-frame word agrees with the dynamic program to numerical precision. A separate signed-area test verifies reversal. Existing tests still pass: 40 total. This verifies the finite implementation, not the physiological interpretation of its states.

## A measurable movement opportunity

Seven prespecified one-second descriptors were assessed on 38 first/last same-player, same-drill attempt pairs from 30 players. Spearman correlations were 0.619 for speed–acceleration area, 0.564 for speed–turn-rate area, 0.679 for acceleration–turn-rate area, and 0.618/0.722/0.376/0.380 for approach/brake/redirect/recover occupancy. These point correlations concern repeated attempts, with some players contributing more than one drill pair. They are not independent-player confidence intervals, intervention results, or predictive gains. All seven are reported rather than selecting the strongest metric after seeing outcomes.

The stronger repeatability of braking measurements than redirect/recovery measurements supports a concrete next development direction: improve angular-phase alignment and measure execution at matched entry speed and angle. The existing first local peak can be an initial adjustment rather than the full semantic route break, and source/game demand mismatch persists. An angular-progress clock, route-specific complete-break segmentation, and repeatability-qualified low-dimensional source channels should be evaluated on earlier seasons before any further forward check. More feature dimensions alone have not produced an advantage. A new untouched class or prospectively reserved population is needed for confirmatory claims after this adaptive program.

## Reproduction and scope

`Ordered_Motion_Channels.ipynb` embeds the current source modules, checks all nine acquisition hashes, reconstructs both original anchors and full follow-up from official CSVs, extracts ordered Combine features, reruns both turn trials and both broad-catch trials, and renders aggregate figures. The local CLI starts with `ordered_motion.py` after the established turn extraction; `ordered_diagnostics.py` recreates repeatability. The notebook deliberately produces no public individual records. Source CSVs, credentials and private intermediates are excluded from the ZIP. No competition entry has been published or submitted. The experiment adds machinery and quantitative evidence; it does not demonstrate a substantial Combine-to-NFL predictive gain or guarantee a prize.
