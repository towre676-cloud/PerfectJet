The strongest positive finding is an observation-channel result: using NFL movement through 0.5 seconds improved prediction of mean speed at 1.3–1.5 seconds. MSE decreased from 0.97136 to 0.64728 in 18 players from 2025 (33.36%). Earlier-player nested validation decreased MSE from 0.96565 to 0.70890 (26.59%). The target is absolute future speed, so this comparison avoids subtracting an input speed from the target. It does not establish that Combine tracking improves pre-draft scouting.

The Combine-specific comparisons remain uncertain or negative. A small sprint-feature gain did not survive repeated player partitions convincingly, and shuffled training profiles performed better on average. Explicit response transport, repeatability-weighted features, receiver separation, and the new defensive-back direction-change test did not establish a reliable incremental improvement. These results remain part of the research record.

The WR cohort contained 23/21/18 rookie players and 4,765/4,337/3,392 eligible routes in 2023/2024/2025. The sprint dictionary used nonlinear speed-response fits, acceleration profiles, Legendre coefficients, and physical-attribute residualization. Candidate models used conventional physical and situational baselines, player-separated nested tuning, and a season-forward comparison. Target-conditioned PLS and unsupervised PCA were compared under matched candidate budgets. Repeated partitions and whole-player profile shuffles challenged the initial finding. Recorded-play eligibility and charted situational attributes limit interpretation as a real-time forecasting application; vendor preprocessing is not fully characterized. WR 2025 comparisons are exploratory after repeated inspection.

The defensive-back revision used CB/FS/SS athletes, four fixed transition drills, and speed/curve features. Its game window began at the first eligible direction change between 0.5 and 2 seconds, with inputs ending at that change and the outcome measured approximately one second later. The initial orientation-based backpedal protocol failed development feasibility and was preserved as a negative result. The revised cohort had 19/27/20 players and 807/1,390/1,032 windows. 2025 motion MSE was 1.19348 versus conventional MSE 1.18279. Development model settings were selected before extracting this DB target, but the original freeze file did not survive maintenance. No new blind-test claim can be made from rerunning this analysis.
