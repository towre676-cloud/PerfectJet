## Source movement and meaningful demand

The unchanged cohort contains 74 defensive-front rookies and 14,665 eligible charted records. Full Combine attempts, rather than only a first-five-second phase model, yield 601 initial direction-change windows from 361 skill attempts. Flanking geometric velocities must support reliable headings, entry speed must exceed 2 yards/s, and net angle must lie between 30° and 170°. Candidate peaks are separated by 1.2 seconds. The candidate definition is operational, not a hand-labeled semantic plant.

For each turn, evaluate 5/7/9-frame cubic smoothing and five centre shifts spanning ±0.2 seconds. Retain at least five valid branches. Incoming/outgoing heading transport removes position/orientation dependence; reflection puts the exit lateral component on the same side for visual comparison. The initial recovery measure starts centre+0.4 seconds. The adaptive version uses monotone estimated angular progress, measures entry before 20% rotation, and begins recovery at 80%. Low-speed heading is interpolated. Reversals, incomplete windows and unreliable flanks are rejected by fixed source-only rules.

For six projected-velocity samples v and trapezoid weights w=(0.05,0.1,0.1,0.1,0.1,0.05), define

\[
\eta=\frac{w^Tv}{0.5s_{entry}}.
\]

This is normalized useful travel over the following 0.5 seconds, not a force, energy or proven motor-efficiency parameter. Retention and later normalized speed gain are supporting channels. Angular-progress alignment retains 582 turns: 135 pass-rush, 226 hoop, 70 four-bag and 151 lateral-reaction, covering all 74 players. Median entry speed is 3.45 yards/s and median angle about 77.4°.

On the same 582 turns, median η sensitivity span falls from 0.283808 to 0.187008, a **34.11% reduction**. This demonstrates narrower variation across the declared processing scenarios; it does not validate the semantic clock or imply a 34% predictive improvement. The scenario set is not a calibrated sensor-error posterior.

Combining numerator bounds with positive entry-speed extrema bounds the normalized ratio. All evaluated branches lie within these declared boxes. Correlations are discarded conservatively; coverage does not extend to arbitrary unmodeled sensor or physiological errors.

Twenty source-only most-ambiguous windows are also represented as six-frame absorbing machines. The readout is projected velocity normalized by entry speed, rounded at 0.05 resolution. The unchanged finite compiler and replay preserve all future readouts of these declared machines. Across these selected examples, 1,800 states reduce to 990. Of 423 branch pairs sharing the initial quantized readout, 290 differ at a later readout. Quantization changes the weighted recovery readout by at most 0.025. This is an observation-order calculation, not a universal runtime gain or equivalence of unseen athletes.

After matching similar prospects by Combine position, forty within 0.10 seconds and weight within 20 lb, the finite matched-recovery intervals overlap in 752 of 864 pair/drill comparisons. Pass-rush overlap is 95.5%, hoop 94.0%, four-bag 91.0%, lateral-reaction 66.0%. These describe sensitivity ambiguity under the earlier-trained source reference, not a probability of misranking NFL talent. ## Why an individual threshold is usually not identified

Consider the modest bilinear response model η=β₀+β₁s+β₂a+β₃sa. With design rows φ=(1,s,a,sa), observations identify a new readout hβ exactly only if h lies in the observed row span. Otherwise a nullspace direction n satisfies Xn=0 but hn≠0: β and β+tn agree on recorded conditions and differ at the new demand. Priors or coefficient restrictions may bound or select a curve; they do not add observations.

Across 278 player/drill panels, design ranks are 108 rank-one, 78 rank-two, 73 rank-three and 19 rank-four. **93.17% do not identify all four coefficients**, and 92.81% leave the reference readout at 4 yards/s and 1.6 radians unidentified. Exact rational calculations round demands to 0.1; unrounded numerical rank gives the same overall deficient fraction. This is a model-specific structural finding, not impossibility of every pooled predictor or nonlinear athlete model.

Complete enumeration of all 512 subsets of nine proposed speed/angle conditions finds minimum declared-cost full-rank plans for twelve example panels. Depending on observed rank, one to three additional independent conditions suffice in those examples. Costs and feasible conditions are illustrative evaluation assumptions, not proven safe/optimal player-development interventions. More frames at the same condition cannot replace missing independent demands.

## Canonical implication and reproducibility

The measured advances are a more stable operational recovery readout and a concrete map of missing evaluations. They narrow the scouting objective: validate turn semantics, collect independent demand conditions and repeats, then evaluate on independent athletes/outcomes. The canonical duration-combined 3.83%/4.49% point lead remains a different exploratory benchmark; this push does not explain it through a verified recovery mechanism or erase its uncertainty.
