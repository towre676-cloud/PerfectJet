# Movement phase populations: an exact calculation and an exploratory transfer test

October 14, 2026. This extends the PerfectPower finite-language adaptation documented in `ORDERED_MOTION.md`. It sums weighted constrained movement histories instead of selecting a single segmentation. The contribution is a reproducible real-valued sum-product calculation and a fair numerical test of its football value.

## Population and measurement

The fixed defensive-front NFL cohort contains 74 DE/DT/NT/OLB rookies, 45 from 2023/2024 and 29 from 2025, with 14,665 eligible charted regular-season rush records. The source filter yields 361 skill attempts from those same 74 athletes: 143 pass-rush, 76 hoop, 71 four-bag and 71 lateral-reaction. For each attempt, retain the first up-to-five seconds after three consecutive speeds above each of 0.5, 1, 1.5 and 2 yards/s. Reject irregular sampling or nonfinite channels. The median span of these onsets is 0.5 seconds, the 90th percentile 1.7 seconds and the maximum 3.8 seconds. This is substantial compared with a 0.1-second sampling interval. Seven-frame cubic position smoothing supplies velocity direction; supplied speed supplies tangential acceleration by differentiation. Suppress angular rates below geometric speed 1 yard/s and cap absolute rotation at 4π radians/s. These operational decisions do not identify the official starting cue or plant frame.

Use normalized channels z=(s/10, (ds/dt)/10, min(|dθ/dt|,4π)/π). Rotation/reflection/translation of the coordinates preserves these channels. Acceleration here is tangential speed change, not the magnitude of the supplied acceleration field, and no physical force or energy is inferred from the geometric proxies.

## Weighted constrained histories

Let q_t be one of four phases: approach, brake, redirect and recover. Allow only q_t−q_(t−1) in {0,1}, require q_0=0 and q_(n−1)=3, and give every permitted path equal base weight. There are C(n−1,3) paths, up to 19,600 in a 51-frame window. The base path measure induces a duration prior; it is not a uniform prior over physical athletes or an empirically identified duration distribution.

For normalized acceleration a and rotation w, use costs

\[
c_t(k)=\frac{1}{2T}\{a_t^2+w_t^2,\;(a_t+0.5)^2+w_t^2,\;a_t^2+(w_t-0.5)^2,\;(a_t-0.5)^2+w_t^2\}_k.
\]

A path's weight is exp(−Σ_t c_t(q_t)). The forward recurrence F_t(k)=exp(−c_t(k))[F_(t−1)(k)+F_(t−1)(k−1)], with forbidden transitions set to zero, computes the partition Z=F_(n−1)(3). The matching backward recurrence gives frame marginals p_t(k)=F_t(k)B_t(k)/Z. Implement both in log space. Complexity is linear in frames and states; exact enumeration on a small trace independently verifies the result.

Features include four expected occupancy fractions, three phase-conditioned channel means per phase, and mean frame-occupancy entropy. This entropy measures ambiguity of individual frame assignments, not the entropy of complete paths. Three signed channel areas, one for each pair of speed, acceleration and rotation, measure chronology:

\[
A_{ij}=\frac12\sum_t\bigl[\bar z_{t,i}\Delta z_{t,j}-\bar z_{t,j}\Delta z_{t,i}\bigr].
\]

Evaluate T=0.5,1,2 for each valid onset and aggregate means and standard deviations with equal scenario weights. Phase weights and onset/temperature weights are explicit sensitivity assumptions, **not calibrated probabilities of semantic phase truth**. Multiple turns and setup movement can violate this single-transition language. Source profiles are attempt medians per player/drill. The full panel has 432 coordinates: 112 summary, 160 sequence and 160 shuffled-control coordinates. With 26 sprint coordinates, predictive families contain 138 summary coordinates or 298 summary-plus-sequence/control coordinates before fold-local compression.

Common summaries already contain timed speeds, geometry and one speed/acceleration signed area. Thus this is a strong temporal baseline. A joint frame permutation changes the added phase and multichannel-area features while preserving each window's channel multiset and leaving the common summaries untouched. This tests incremental sequence information, not whether all movement chronology matters. Permutations change local smoothness and there is only one fixed permutation per window; no exchangeability-certified significance test is claimed.

## Mathematical synthesis

Our first source engine uses framewise stay/advance paths over approach, brake, redirect and recovery, several onset thresholds and phase-cost temperatures. It computes expected occupancies, phase-conditioned motion, signed channel areas and sensitivity spreads. The duration engine assigns contiguous intervals to ordered phases, respecting positive minimum/maximum duration and optional boundary windows. It supports exact population access and numerical observations on the same finite graph.

For an edge e from v to u with log weight ell_e and reward r_e, integer suffix counts and weighted masses satisfy

\[
C(v)=\sum_{e:v\to u}C(u),\qquad Z(v)=\sum_{e:v\to u}\exp(\ell_e)Z(u).
\]

With p_e=exp(ell_e)Z(u)/Z(v), suffix mean and variance obey

\[
\mu(v)=\sum_e p_e[r_e+\mu(u)],\qquad
V(v)=\sum_e p_e\{V(u)+[r_e+\mu(u)-\mu(v)]^2\}.
\]

The stable central-variance recurrence avoids subtracting nearly equal large raw moments. Min/max reward recurrences give deterministic bounds over the accepted language. Forward/suffix masses give boundary and frame marginals. Rank and inverse rank use exact integer suffix blocks; weighted sampling uses numerical suffix masses. Rational graph inputs also admit an exact Fraction reference API. These statements concern the specified finite language, not every physically possible movement.

Tensor signatures preserve event order through Chen concatenation of piecewise-linear increments. The donor adapter retains second-order areas and six third-order motion-coordinate permutations; it does not inject prime arithmetic or quantum dynamics into physiology. Operational phase templates, fixed normalization scales, smoothing, duration constraints and equal onset weights remain assumptions. Exact inference can faithfully analyze a physically poor model. A deterministic support range is not a statistical confidence interval, and a model boundary quantile is not an empirically calibrated plant-frame confidence interval.

The donor source accepts 1,083 onset scenarios from 361 attempts, with 3,276–27,720 segmentations per scenario. A synthetic 300-frame/12-phase graph contains 35,513,096,590,299,098,589 segmentations, handled by 435,754 graph edges. That is a scalability result on synthetic data, not a scouting-accuracy result.

## Why an individual threshold is usually not identified

Consider the modest bilinear response model η=β₀+β₁s+β₂a+β₃sa. With design rows φ=(1,s,a,sa), observations identify a new readout hβ exactly only if h lies in the observed row span. Otherwise a nullspace direction n satisfies Xn=0 but hn≠0: β and β+tn agree on recorded conditions and differ at the new demand. Priors or coefficient restrictions may bound or select a curve; they do not add observations.

Across 278 player/drill panels, design ranks are 108 rank-one, 78 rank-two, 73 rank-three and 19 rank-four. **93.17% do not identify all four coefficients**, and 92.81% leave the reference readout at 4 yards/s and 1.6 radians unidentified. Exact rational calculations round demands to 0.1; unrounded numerical rank gives the same overall deficient fraction. This is a model-specific structural finding, not impossibility of every pooled predictor or nonlinear athlete model.

Complete enumeration of all 512 subsets of nine proposed speed/angle conditions finds minimum declared-cost full-rank plans for twelve example panels. Depending on observed rank, one to three additional independent conditions suffice in those examples. Costs and feasible conditions are illustrative evaluation assumptions, not proven safe/optimal player-development interventions. More frames at the same condition cannot replace missing independent demands.

## Baseline-preserving corrections

A high-shrinkage follow-up changed conventional coefficients too; it remains a diagnostic. The corrected experiment fixes conventional penalty 100 and added-motion penalty 1000. Include a free intercept in$B$, conventional penalty matrix$P_B$, and $A=B^TB+P_B$. Then

$$\beta_0=A^{-1}B^Ty,\quad T=A^{-1}B^TZ,$$

$$\gamma=(Z^TZ-Z^TBT+1000 I)^{-1}Z^T(y-B\beta_0),$$

$$\widehat y_*=B_*\beta_0+(Z_*-B_*T)\gamma.$$

Schur elimination equals the full block-penalized solve. Zero motion returns the original prediction exactly; independent numerical tests check both facts. No runtime superiority claim follows from this small calculation.

Completely enumerate nonnegative correction triples from 0,0.1,0.25,0.5,1 with sum at most one.
