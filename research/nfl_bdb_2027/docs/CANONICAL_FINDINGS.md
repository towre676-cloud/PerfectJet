## Mathematical synthesis

Our first source engine uses framewise stay/advance paths over approach, brake, redirect and recovery, several onset thresholds and phase-cost temperatures. It computes expected occupancies, phase-conditioned motion, signed channel areas and sensitivity spreads. The duration engine assigns contiguous intervals to ordered phases, respecting positive minimum/maximum duration and optional boundary windows. It supports exact population access and numerical observations on the same finite graph.

For an edge e from v to u with log weight ell_e and reward r_e, integer suffix counts and weighted masses satisfy

\[
C(v)=\sum_{e:v\to u}C(u),\qquad Z(v)=\sum_{e:v\to u}\exp(\ell_e)Z(u).
\]

With p_e=exp(ell_e)Z(u)/Z(v), suffix mean and variance obey

\[
\mu(v)=\sum_e p_e[r_e+\mu(u)],\qquad
V(v)=\sum_e p_e\{V(u)+[r_e+\mu(u)-\mu(v)]^2\}.
\]

The stable central-variance recurrence avoids subtracting nearly equal large raw moments. Min/max reward recurrences give deterministic bounds over the accepted language. Forward/suffix masses give boundary and frame marginals. Rank and inverse rank use exact integer suffix blocks; weighted sampling uses numerical suffix masses. Rational graph inputs also admit an exact Fraction reference API. These statements concern the specified finite language, not every physically possible movement.

Tensor signatures preserve event order through Chen concatenation of piecewise-linear increments. The donor adapter retains second-order areas and six third-order motion-coordinate permutations; it does not inject prime arithmetic or quantum dynamics into physiology. Operational phase templates, fixed normalization scales, smoothing, duration constraints and equal onset weights remain assumptions. Exact inference can faithfully analyze a physically poor model. A deterministic support range is not a statistical confidence interval, and a model boundary quantile is not an empirically calibrated plant-frame confidence interval.

The donor source accepts 1,083 onset scenarios from 361 attempts, with 3,276–27,720 segmentations per scenario. A synthetic 300-frame/12-phase graph contains 35,513,096,590,299,098,589 segmentations, handled by 435,754 graph edges. That is a scalability result on synthetic data, not a scouting-accuracy result.

## Training uncertainty and interpretation

Five hundred twelve class-stratified training-player bootstraps refit all imputers, scales, position encoding, physical residualization, PCA and the conventional/duration-combined regressions. Configurations remain fixed at their earlier-selected values. The fixed-test-player interval for absolute gain is[−0.000168918,0.000109654]. Joint training and test-player resampling gives[−0.000206879,0.000140302]. These intervals include meaningful losses; they do not repeat complete model selection or adjust the adaptive program. The three repeated partitions also reuse the same athletes and cannot be treated as independent sample-size multiplication.

The canonical empirical finding is a **small positive duration-combined point signal that remains unconfirmed under broader uncertainty**, alongside a well-tested mathematical observation engine. Kinematics, order and uncertainty together are a useful hypothesis; the comparisons do not isolate causal interactions or prove that all three components are necessary. PCA changes its basis when the source set changes, so family gains are package comparisons, not a theorem about one physical mechanism.

The next scientific objective is a repeatable, drill-specific channel with validated phase semantics and independently reserved outcomes. Complete-turn matching should condition source measurements on entry speed and turn angle; it should examine acceleration after the turn at matched demand and quantify repeated-attempt stability. Calibration of phase weights needs annotations or an independently validated sensor-error model. A proposed coaching intervention needs causal evidence. Another sweep against this already-inspected 2025 class cannot supply fresh confirmation.
