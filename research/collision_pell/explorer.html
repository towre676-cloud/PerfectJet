03The other side of the numerator

Invert 1 + 2x − x². Its coefficients are signed, shifted Pell numbers.

Exact powering
1 / (1 + 2x − x²) = ∑n≥0 (−1)nPn+1 xn

P₀ = 0, P₁ = 1, and Pn+2 = 2Pn+1 + Pₙ. The reciprocal series converges for |x| < √2 − 1.
