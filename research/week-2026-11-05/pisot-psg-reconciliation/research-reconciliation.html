1 / Current mathematical position

The later recoveries supply the organizing principle

The current PerfectJet release is v0.21.0, published on 31 October 2026. Its 744 equations, 27 families and 30 tools supersede the October v0.17 monograph as a release inventory. More useful than the inventory is the mathematical separation already present in its current source: target observability, exact closure, residual repair, and parameter sensitivity. These supply a precise language for bringing the other projects together. [1] [2] [3]

For linear measurements Ox and a requested target Gx, exact recovery is equivalent to

kerO⊆kerG⟺G=LO.

The forward implication defines the map L on the image of O; the kernel inclusion makes that definition independent of the chosen preimage. Conversely, a factorization makes the inclusion immediate. A state component may be unobservable while the requested target remains exactly recoverable. Increasing the family of requested targets can require a larger representation.

For polynomial features p and an update F, the separate question is whether

p∘F=Gp.

If this identity holds, the feature evolution closes exactly. If it fails, the nonzero residual identifies information the representation loses. Exact output recovery at one point and exact dynamic closure are distinct requirements. The later source implements both checks, including the possibility that no finite augmentation closes the chosen class. [3]

2 / An exact recovered identity

The logarithmic integral is exact

Set

W(x)=−log(cosx)+ix,J(α)=∫0π/2Re(W(x)−α)dx.

All logarithms and powers use their principal branches. The original transcripts alternate between an exact evaluation and a later assertion that the expression only approximates a residue contribution. The following proof resolves that disagreement. [6] [7]

Proposition 1 · exact evaluation
J(α)=π2(log2)α,0<α<1.

Proof. Define on the open unit disk

H(z)=log2−Log(1+z),Fα(z)=H(z)−α.

Since |1+z|<2, the real part of H is positive. Thus Fα is holomorphic. Its circular mean equals Fα(0)=(log2)−α. Near z=1,

H(z)=1−z2+O((1−z)2).

For radial approach, the resulting boundary singularity is bounded by a constant times |θ|−α, which is integrable when α<1. Near z=−1, the real part of H tends to positive infinity and the power tends to zero. Dominated convergence therefore passes the circular mean to the boundary. On the lower semicircle parameterized by z=e−2ix,

H(e−2ix)=−log(cosx)+ix=W(x).

Conjugate symmetry identifies the real half-interval integral with one quarter of the full angular integral. The result follows. This argument also makes clear why the arithmetic type of a base parameter cannot alter the answer.

The endpoint carries a real boundary correction

The formula is stated on its proper open parameter range. At α=1, the integral of the real part exists, while the complex integral itself diverges at the origin. Subtract the boundary pole:

R(z)=1H(z)−21−z.

The expansion 1/H(z)=2/(1−z)−1/2+O(1−z) makes R continuous at z=1. It is also continuous at z=−1, with value −1. Hence its boundary mean is R(0)=1/log2−2. Since Re[2/(1−e−2ix)]=1,

J(1)=π2log2−π2=0.69538374411870028558…,limα↑1J(α)=π2log2.

The difference is π/2. Endpoint concentration explains why unspecialized quadrature can suggest a false failure of the interior identity. The supplied numerical check subtracts the lower-end singular term and uses a logarithmic variable for the opposite endpoint. Six interior values, including α=0.99, agree with the analytic formula in independent 80- and 100-digit calculations. These quadratures support the proof; they are not interval certificates.

The quotient has an immediate consequence

When α=β−t, with β>1 and t>0, both this integral and the scalar map below depend only on α. The normalized integral is identically

2π(log2)β−t∫0π/2Re(W(x)−β−t)dx=1.

Choosing a Pisot or Salem value for β labels a curve in the original parameter plane; the conjugates of that number do not enter this scalar identity. Genuine arithmetic information requires an object that actually retains those conjugates.

3 / A certified stability boundary

One flip, with the branch on the correct side

Let

L(x)=−log(cosx),U(x)=x2+L(x)2,Kα(x)=U(x)−α/2,0<x<π/2.

This is the modulus of the complex power, not its real part. With H1=x+Ltanx, its derivative is Kα′=−αH1Kα/U<0. The map decreases from positive infinity to zero and therefore has a unique fixed point for every α>0. The whole interval is not invariant under the map; the conclusions below concern local fixed-point and cycle geometry.

Proposition 2 · interval-assisted uniqueness

There is exactly one positive parameter at which the fixed-point multiplier equals −1. It lies in (0,1), and

α*=0.74180451508789468891901626577308272417…,x*=0.94285767998233843967338162657360719637….

The fixed point is locally attracting below α* and repelling above it.

Proof and certificate. Let x0 solve U(x0)=1. Every positive-parameter fixed point lies in (x0,1), where

α(x)=−2logxlogU(x)

decreases strictly from infinity to zero. The flip equation reduces to

G(x)=UlogU+2xH1logx=0.

Both terms are negative on (0,x0], and G is positive on [1,π/2). The certificate proves U(0.887)<1, then covers [0.887,1] by 1,130 exact rational cells of width 10−4. Outward-rounded Arb arithmetic at 384 bits proves G′>4.66 on every cell. Strict opposite signs at the two exact decimal bracket endpoints enclose the unique root. All endpoints and interval outputs are retained in pisot-scalar-certificate.json.

The later transcript already had the corrected root digits and a correct positive cubic coefficient of the second iterate. Its real branch formula nevertheless had the wrong sign. Current PerfectJet correctly limited its implementation to floating replay; the new certificate closes the uniqueness question for the declared kernel. [7] [4]

4 / The precise content of stability equivalence

A ratio profile determines a family up to parameter scale

The PSG synthesis idea survives with a corrected sign and domain. On a connected interval I⊂(0,1), take a positive smooth potential Φ with nonzero derivative, and define

Kα(x)=e−αΦ(x),RΦ(x)=Φ(x)xΦ′(x).

At a fixed point, α=−logx/Φ(x). Direct substitution gives

μ(x)=logxRΦ(x).

The minus sign in the recovered draft's numerator would reverse the orientation and must be corrected. [8]

Proposition 3 · exact synthesis and equivalence

For a smooth prescribed profile ν(x)<0,

Φ(x)=cexp(∫xrefxν(t)tlogtdt),c>0,

realizes that fixed-point multiplier on the parameter image of I. Moreover,

RΦ1=RΦ2⟺Φ2=cΦ1⟹Kα(2)=Kcα(1).

Proof. Differentiating the synthesis formula gives xΦ′/Φ=ν/logx, hence μ=ν. Along the fixed branch, α′(x)=(ν−1)/(xΦ)<0, so the parameterization is invertible on its image. Equality of the ratio functions gives equality of logarithmic derivatives of the two positive potentials; integration on the connected interval gives a positive constant ratio.

If a unique threshold exists, normalization by u=α/α* removes that constant parameter scale. This is a precise equivalence result in a fixed state coordinate. Merely arranging equal threshold locations is weaker and does not identify two families.

Equal thresholds can hide opposite bifurcations

For Φ=cxr, with c,r>0, the principal Lambert function gives

μ(α)=−W0(αcr),α*=ecr,μ(u)=−W0(eu).

At x*=e−1/r, write the centered map at the flip as −y+a2y2+a3y3+⋯. Its normal-form cubic coefficient is

a3+a22=r212x*2>0.

For the logarithmic kernel the corresponding coefficient is −σ2/2<0. Choosing the amplitude c can align the threshold values while leaving these signs opposite. This gives an explicit reason to retain nonlinear local data when comparing stability laws.

The reserve ρ=1−|μ| is a useful margin, but it is determined by μ. A numerical amplitude coefficient also depends on state normalization: under ynew=sy, its magnitude scales by |s|, while the cubic coefficient scales by s−2. A defensible ψ description therefore specifies its permitted coordinate changes and the information it preserves.

5 / Arithmetic that genuinely remains

Conjugate contraction belongs to a specified spectrum

For a Pisot algebraic integer β=β1>1, with conjugates β2,…,βd, put r=maxj≥2|βj|<1. An integer companion matrix C gives the elementary exact mechanism

Nn=tr(Cn)=∑j=1dβjn∈Z,|βn−Nn|≤(d−1)rn.

The trace certifies exponentially close integer approximation. It concerns the conjugates in that irreducible polynomial. A different operator with the same leading eigenvalue can contain additional expanding modes.

7 / One coherent contribution

Preserve the answer; measure the cost of discarded information

The common contribution can be stated precisely: identify an exact quotient where one exists, expose the failure of closure where it does not, and bound the effect on the requested observable. This is a shared mathematical question with different answers in different models.

The scalar Pisot reduction is complete because the original parameters enter through one exponent. The ratio-profile theorem says exactly when two stability potentials differ only by parameter scale. The finite graph admits an exact symmetry quotient for symmetric observations. The GIM coefficient requires its physically weighted small mode. The flavor counterterm produces an explicit nonzero polynomial obstruction. The annulus publication establishes which continued functions and extension directions a difficult physical period problem must retain.

These statements should be presented as distinct theorems and examples under that common question. The dimensions 1, 2, 3, 37 and 114 refer to different objects and categories; there is no legitimate operation that equates them simply because each describes a form of reduction. Likewise, the Asterion half-braid work contributes methods for exact continuation and branch conventions, while its KZ system retains its own mathematical identity. [15]

The current unequal-mass milestone remains the appropriate physical focus. Its two linked tests are a known mixed-mass flavor kernel with a coefficient-level error budget, and one certified nonzero mass deformation of the crossed box anchored by J64. The additional recoveries strengthen the acceptance criteria: include parameter response, verify the operative branch, and distinguish exact reductions from approximations. They do not require adding a separate GUT, tiling, or arithmetic-classification project.

The practical value is a way to decide which parts of a complicated calculation are essential to its answer. Exact structure removes genuine redundancy. A closure defect identifies missing information. An observable bound states what an approximation costs.
