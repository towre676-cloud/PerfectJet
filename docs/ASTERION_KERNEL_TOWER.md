# Higher E8 arc kernels, translation towers and finite characters

This module continues the exact weight-four calculation in
[ASTERION_RELATIONS.md](ASTERION_RELATIONS.md). The new conclusions concern
the canonical map from the arc algebra of the level-one E8 C2 algebra to
the Li associated graded of the simple affine vertex algebra. They do not
give an infinite presentation of its kernel, or a new definition of E8.
World-first priority has not been established.

The computations use rational and integer arithmetic in the Chevalley
coordinates already fixed by the package. No external algebra system or
large tensor-product table is needed.

## Operators and the all-order translation theorem

Write the divided jet variables as x^[r]=D^r x/r!. Thus

    D x^[r]=(r+1)x^[r+1],  E x^[r]=x^[r-1],  E x^[0]=0.

Both extend as derivations on the polynomial algebra. Directly on each
variable, [E,D]=N, where N counts ordinary polynomial degree. If q is an
ordinary homogeneous relation of degree p, then

    E(D^s q)=s p D^(s-1)q.

Consequently E preserves the differential ideal of the ordinary relations
and descends to the arc algebra. It is locally nilpotent on every finite
jet polynomial. D is injective on every positive ordinary-degree component
of the arc algebra: if Dv=0 and Nv=pv with p>0, then

    D^r E^r v=(-p)^r r! v.

For large enough r the left side vanishes, so v=0 in characteristic zero.
This proof works for any ordinary homogeneous ideal; it is not specific to
E8. E is asserted to act on the source arc algebra. We do not assume it
descends to the target associated graded or preserves the arc-map kernel.
D preserves that kernel because the canonical map is differential.

For the previously certified weight-four generator F_Q, ordinary degree is
three and E F_Q=0. The latter identity is checked on all783 terms and also
follows from contraction of an infinitesimal adjoint action with the
invariant quadratic tensor. Hence

    E^r D^r F_Q=3^r r! F_Q,  r>=0.

This is an explicit left inverse for every translated3875-dimensional
module. The first checks have783,1566,2913 monomials in conformal
weights4,5,6, with Li degrees1,2,3 and inverse scalars1,3,18. The identity
proves nonzero kernel classes at every weight at least four; a finite
derivative panel is only a replay of this all-order proof.

The prior imported facts are stated explicitly in the receipt: F_Q is a
nonzero class of the old arc algebra, and it generates the complete
weight-four V3875 kernel. The full-sector PBW null-lift and nonmembership
certificate remain in the earlier relations module.

## Annihilators and differential syzygies

The previous projected-polarization certificate proves
I27000*g=Sym^3(g). Every ordinary cubic in zero jets is therefore zero in
the arc algebra. F_Q contains precisely two zero-jet variables and one
first jet in every monomial. It follows that

    x^[0] F_Q=0 for every x in g.

The same holds for its whole finite E8 orbit. Differentiating gives the
exact family of syzygies

    sum_(j=0)^r binom(r,j) j! x^[j] D^(r-j)F_Q=0.

The program verifies the Leibniz coefficients as polynomial identities
before passing to the arc quotient. At weight five, the commutative
differential ideal generated by K4 contributes only D(K4): its product
g[0]K4 is zero. Injectivity of D proves dim D(K4)=3875. This is a
statement about the graded commutative differential ideal. It is not a
claim that these are the strong generators of an unfiltered vertex
algebra, nor a complete syzygy presentation.

## Exact finite E8 characters

`E8Freudenthal` reconstructs finite irreducible characters from the exact
Freudenthal recursion, folding every upward weight into the dominant Weyl
chamber. From a dominant mu and positive root alpha,

    ||mu+k alpha||^2=||mu||^2+2k(mu,alpha)+2k^2

strictly increases for k>0. Thus the bounded recursion is acyclic. The
highest weight bound is a norm bound, not a cutoff on unexplored recursive
depth. E8 has integral inverse Cartan matrix; dominant Dynkin labels are
enumerated exhaustively using its positive diagonal bounds.

The E8 lattice realization of the level-one vacuum algebra gives the
finite-weight multiplicity

    m_n(mu)=p8(n-||mu||^2/2).

The engine subtracts higher finite irreducibles at every dominant weight
of norm at most2n. Weyl dimensions then independently reconstruct the

Finite characters alone do not locate a kernel in the Li filtration. We
therefore separately certify the full at-most-two-current piece at weights
five and six. The symmetric square is exhausted by1+3875+27000; the
exterior square by248+30380. Exact finite highest vectors are computed,
their Weyl dimensions sum to the ambient squares, and all allocations of
two creation modes are included. The adjoint block also includes the

## Finite closure and its boundary

The weight-five arc character is certified separately by the S3
pair-restriction calculation in `asterion_kernel_characters`. Its
length-three/Li-degree-two component has dimension1112621; the remaining
short-current component has dimension96256. Length four and higher
vanish because of the ordinary cubic relations. Combining that exact
character with the independent lattice/Freudenthal character gives

    A5=2·1+6·248+4·3875+27000+3·30380+2·147250+779247,
    K5=248+3875+147250,  dim K5=151373.

All K5 is in Li degree two by the short-current injection theorem.
The quotient by the differential ideal generated at lower weight is

    K5/(D K4+g[0]K4)=248+147250,  dimension147498.

This disproves differential generation of the full kernel by its
weight-four module alone. D injectivity further proves a kernel dimension
of at least151373 at every weight at least five.

The finite character identities and the previously certified Jordan
twining series also give an exact density refinement. Each nonidentity
order-five automorphism has traces−2,0,0,5,0 on
248,3875,27000,30380,147250 respectively. The3875 and147250
modules restrict to multiples of the125-dimensional regular Jordan
representation. Fourier projection gives:

| Module | Neutral grade | Each of124 nonzero grades |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
|K5=248+3875+147250|1209|1211|
|Primitive quotient248+147250|1178|1180|

D preserves every Jordan grade, so the first row is also a lower bound
at every later weight. The traces are derived from V2,V4,Sym^2(g) and
exterior^2(g), with all scalar coefficients exposed in the receipt.

At weight six, the exact length-three arc Hilbert coefficient4585365
and the injective short-current dimension127132 already give

    dim K6[ordinary degree3] >=4585365-(4530744-127132)=181753.

The full finite-character calculation strengthens this bound. The
length-three arc character is

    1+5·248+4·3875+27000+3·30380+3·147250+2·779247+2450240.

Subtracting the injective short-current character
3·1+3·248+3·3875+2·27000+2·30380 from the total simple
character bounds each remaining target multiplicity. Consequently the
Li-degree-three kernel contains a submodule

    248+3875+30380+147250.

This exceeds the151373-dimensional translated K5 by a forced V30380
summand. Products of any earlier kernel class with a positive-weight
variable have ordinary degree at least four and cannot fill this
homogeneous degree-three component. Thus a new V30380 differential-ideal
primitive is required at weight six, beyond every generator through weight
five. Its Jordan restriction supplies at least248 neutral and243 in each
nonzero grade. This is a submodule lower bound, not an explicit new PBW
null vector or the complete weight-six primitive character. The exact
total weight-six kernel and its full higher-length sectors require
additional work. No infinite closure is inferred from these finite
statements.

## API, bounds and provenance

Run the focused panel with:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src python -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_asterion_kernel_tower.py -v
```

`kernel_tower_receipt(exhaustive=False)` returns a JSON-safe object with a
top-level Boolean `passed`. The exhaustive flag extends explicit finite
translation/syzygy replays. It never means an infinite kernel presentation
has been verified. Character degrees are limited to0..12; the shipped
combined receipt uses0..6. Finite monomial translation replays are bounded
to derivative order8 and syzygy replays to6. Invalid indices, Boolean
indices, malformed highest weights, inexact jet coefficients, oversized
norm requests and non-Boolean flags are rejected before the relevant
allocation. Gram blocks have at most three columns each; the exterior
highest search has an explicit128-column ceiling.

The source of Freudenthal's recursion is classical representation theory;
an explicit primary research treatment is M. Cavallin,
[An algorithm for computing weight multiplicities in irreducible modules
for complex semisimple Lie algebras](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.07196),
Journal of Algebra471(2017),492–510,
[DOI10.1016/j.jalgebra.2016.08.044](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2016.08.044).
The lattice-VOA/arc comparison and the previously known weight-four
nonfreeness are discussed by Song and Zeng,
[arXiv2606.14407, Section6](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14407).
The package's additional finite certificates are mathematical deductions
within these conventions; their global novelty is unverified.
