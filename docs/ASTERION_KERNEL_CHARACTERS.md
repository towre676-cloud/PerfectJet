# Arc characters beyond the first E8 relation

This calculation closes the arc algebra through conformal weight5 and the
entire factor-length-three part at every jet degree. It does not classify
the infinite kernel of the map from the arc algebra to the associated
graded level-one E8 vertex algebra.

Write `g=E8`, `I=V(2 theta)` of dimension27000, and
`W=Sym²(g)/I=1+3875`. The ordinary algebra is

\[
R=\operatorname{Sym}(g)/(I)=1\oplus g\oplus W.
\]

All ordinary cubics vanish. This is established by the exact projected
polarization certificate in `asterion_relations.py`, rather than assumed
from classical freeness. Divided jets are denoted `x[r]`, with conformal
weight `r+1`; the factor length is the number of current variables and the
Li differential degree is the sum of their jet indices.

## Pair relations and polynomial covariants

At factor length3, dualize the finite component of each jet degree `d`.
It becomes the simultaneous `S3` invariants in

\[
g^{\otimes3}\otimes\mathbb Q[t_1,t_2,t_3]_d.
\]

The relation `sum_(r+s=n) I(x[r],x[s])=0` says exactly that the pair-null
projection of the dual polynomial vanishes at `t1=t2`. Simultaneous `S3`
invariance gives the other pair conditions automatically. This equivalence
uses the coefficient pairing for divided jets; it does not replace a Lie
bracket with polynomial multiplication.

Let `s=t1+t2+t3` and `y_i=t_i-s/3`. The symmetric-polynomial ring has
generators `s`, `sum y_i²`, and `sum y_i³`, of degrees1,2,3. The two standard
covariants are the vectors

\[
(y_1,y_2,y_3),\qquad
(y_1^2-\tfrac13\sum y_i^2,
 y_2^2-\tfrac13\sum y_i^2,
 y_3^2-\tfrac13\sum y_i^2).
\]

They are independent over the invariant ring: their determinant together
with the constant vector `(1,1,1)` is a nonzero multiple of the Vandermonde
polynomial. The character/Molien identities show that their free module
has the full standard-covariant Hilbert series `(t+t²)/D`, where
`D=(1-t)(1-t²)(1-t³)`. Thus independence and the Hilbert identity establish
generation at every degree. Alternating polynomials are the Vandermonde
times symmetric polynomials, with Hilbert series `t³/D`.

On the diagonal `(t1,t2,t3)=(t,t,u)`, put `v=t-u`, so
`y=(v/3,v/3,-2v/3)`. The two centered invariants restrict to `2v²/3` and
`-2v³/9`; the two standard covariants restrict along `(1,1,-2)` with
coefficients `v/3` and `-v²/9`. Consequently their images are

| Pair-fixed type | Image | Rank at degree `d` |
|---|---|---:|
| Trivial | `A=Q[s,v²,v³]` | `max(1,d)` |
| Standard | `vA+v²A=v Q[s,v]` | `d` |
| Nonzero mixture | `A+v Q[s,v]=Q[s,v]` | `d+1` |

These are all-degree identities: the semigroup generated by2 and3 is
`{0,2,3,...}`. The finite exact Gaussian elimination panel in the receipt
checks the implementation, but is explicitly not the all-order proof.

## Which pair-fixed type occurs

The multiplicity-free decomposition is

\[
g\otimes I=248+27000+30380+779247+1763125+4096000.
\]

All tensor decompositions used here are independently reconstructed by
`tensor_cube_character_receipt()` in `asterion_kernel_tower.py` from the241
distinct adjoint weights, Newton identities and exact Freudenthal
recursion. Published formulas in Song–Zeng §6.2 provide a cross-check; no
large representation table is accepted merely because its dimensions sum
correctly.

The actual sparse Lie-bracket polarization certificate recomputes
`mu P_I delta` on the four symmetric-cube summands. Its scalars are

| Summand | Scalar | Pair-fixed type |
|---|---:|---|
|248|54/31|Nonzero mixture|
|30380|3/7|Nonzero mixture|
|779247|13/7|Nonzero mixture|
|1763125|3|Pure trivial|

Indeed `mu delta=3`, so the squared projection of the trivial `S3` line
onto the pair-null line is the scalar divided by3. Each of the first three
lies strictly between0 and1. A transposition-fixed vector has only
trivial and standard components; its sign component is zero. The remaining
27000 and4096000 do not occur in the symmetric cube, so their pair-fixed
vectors are pure standard. Multiplicity one means that each restriction
imposes exactly the corresponding one-line image condition.

Thus the removed dimensions in jet degree `d>=1` are

\[
(248+30380+779247)(d+1)+(1763125+27000+4096000)d.
\]

The unconstrained component has character
`a_d Sym³g+b_d S_(2,1)g+c_d exterior³g`, where
`a_d=[t^d]1/D`, `b_d=[t^d](t+t²)/D`, and `c_d=[t^d]t³/D`.
Subtracting the pair-diagonal image gives the exact all-degree answer.
In particular the factor-length-three Hilbert series is

\[
\frac{151373t+961248t^2+3321371t^3+4932875t^4+
4123000t^5+1763125t^6}{(1-t)(1-t^2)(1-t^3)}.
\]

Its first dimensions, starting with jet degree0, are
`0,151373,1112621,4585365`. The receipt also provides the complete E8
character at every requested jet degree using independently reconstructed
`Sym³g`, `S_(2,1)g`, and `exterior³g`.

## Complete weight5 and the next boundary

At weight5, all terms with four or more factors vanish: they contain at
least three zero jets and hence an ordinary cubic. The surviving pieces
are

|---:|---:|---:|
|3|2|1112621|
|2|3|96008|

|Total||1208877|

The complete finite E8 character is

\[
2\cdot1+6\cdot248+4\cdot3875+27000+3\cdot30380+
2\cdot147250+779247.
\]

The level-one lattice character has dimension1057504 at weight5, so the
canonical map's kernel has dimension151373. The differential ideal
generated solely by the first weight4 module can contribute at most3875
at weight5: multiplying its cubic jet representatives by a zero jet
vanishes because all ordinary cubics vanish. Thus at least147498 new
primitive relations are required at weight5. The tower module independently
proves derivative injectivity and reconstructs the actual finite VOA
characters; those additional checks identify the modules precisely.

At weight6, the known factor-length1,2,3 pieces have dimensions
`248,126884,4585365`, totaling4712497. Subtracting the actual VOA dimension
4530744 gives the rigorous kernel lower bound181753. The factor-length4,
Li-degree2 component remains outside this receipt's completeness claim.
It can increase the full arc dimension and its kernel. A separate quartic
boundary calculation may strengthen this bound; it must not be silently
treated as a complete weight6 classification.
