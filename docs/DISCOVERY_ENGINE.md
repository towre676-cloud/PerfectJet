# Discovery engine: three proof-carrying research frontiers

## Parametric Hamiltonian incidence

The shared `parametric_kernel_tower` constructs exact constructible
rank cells for bounded iterates of polynomial correction gates. The first
non-affine sixfold-leading discovery consumer is
`nonlinearhamiltonian/research/hamiltonian/perfectjet_bridge/sixfold_cubic_discovery.py`.

For (H_lambda=q^6+lambda p^3), nonzero tested rational parameters give
bounded (S^0,S^1,S^2) dimensions (15,4,2) on total degree at most nine.
The candidate all-degree core is (mathbf Q[H_lambda]), but the finite
experiment does not establish that claim. The remaining work is a uniform
static-tail theorem.

## Cubic arithmetic certificate scout

`cubic_certificate_search` searches monic integer cubics for:

1. irreducibility (integer-root exclusion);
2. an Eisenstein integral translate at a specified prime;
3. squarefreeness of the residual power-basis discriminant.

Together with standard local Eisenstein maximality and the
discriminant-index formula, these prove that the monogenic power order is the
full ring of integers. The engine also verifies candidate principal elements
of small prime norm by multiplication-matrix determinants.

Examples with discriminants (148,404,788,1076,1556) are replayed in the
Selmer pipeline. These are maximal-order certificates, **not** class-number,
unit-completeness, Kummer, Selmer or Sha certificates.

## Independent proof boundaries

No bounded Hamiltonian calculation proves an all-degree core theorem. No
sensor theorem applies without a specified error-cost model. No power-order
certificate proves class number one or any elliptic-curve descent conclusion.
The research consumers retain independent implementations where possible.
