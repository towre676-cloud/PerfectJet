# An explicit weight-four relation for the level-one E8 arc map

This chapter records an explicit relation found by the Asterion numerical program. It distinguishes a null vector in an affine vacuum module, its leading Li symbol, and nonmembership in the differential consequences of the ordinary C2 relations. An exact projected-polarization calculation then proves that this3875-dimensional finite E8 submodule is the entire conformal-weight-four arc-map kernel. It does not claim that this submodule generates the full infinite differential kernel or that the formula has no prior occurrence in the literature.

The external starting point is Bailin Song and Xianlong Zeng, *Zhu's algebra and the C2-algebra of a classically free vertex operator algebra*, arXiv:2606.14407, submitted12June2026. Section6 proves that L1(E8) is not classically free by a dimension contradiction. Its argument identifies the finite3875 representation as one of the components that differential consequences cannot remove. The present program supplies explicit coordinates, an affine null lift, and independent finite-weight rank certificates for that component. Source: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.14407>.

## Complete weight-four kernel by projected polarization

The calculation also determines the complete conformal-weight-four kernel, rather than only exhibiting a submodule. This uses explicit highest vectors and small exact scalar identities instead of a huge mixed-tensor quotient matrix.

\[

The singular square, its finite E8 action, and its differential consequences are classical and are labelled known. The783-term generator,804-term lift, two source-rank certificates and projected-polarization proof are labelled new to this program, with no historical-priority claim. The complete conformal-weight-four result is proved by the recorded exact identities.
