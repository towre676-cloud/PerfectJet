# The level-four five-channel E8 monodromy group

The exhaustive certificate classifies the closure of the particular five-dimensional KZ representation implemented in `asterion_kz.py`. It uses rigorous loop enclosures and stated representation-theoretic theorems. It does not infer a group from approximate numerical spectra, transfer a generic-parameter result to a rational parameter, or claim historical priority.

Let Gamma be generated by the two based counterclockwise local holonomies U,V for

\[
F'=\frac1{34}\left(\frac A z+\frac B{z-1}\right)F,
\qquad \operatorname{spec}(A)=(-60,-30,-12,0,2).
\]

After a fixed change of basis making the invariant Hermitian form standard, its compact closure is

\[
\overline\Gamma=\{g\in U(5):\det(g)^{17}=1\}.
\]

Thus the identity component is SU(5), there are17 components, and the projective image is dense in PU(5). The complex Zariski closure is exactly

\[
\{g\in GL(5,\mathbb C):\det(g)^{17}=1\},
\]

with identity component SL(5,C). These conclusions are reported as certified in a run only after the actual loop and irreducibility gates pass. Fast receipts explicitly leave that gate open.

## Exact spectrum and determinant

The level-four local indicial exponents have no integral differences. The checked Frobenius basis therefore conjugates U to its diagonal local exponential. For zeta=exp(2*pi*i/17),

\[
\operatorname{spec}(U)=\operatorname{spec}(V)
=\{\zeta^4,\zeta^2,\zeta^{11},1,\zeta\}.
\]

Both local generators have actual and projective order17. Their determinants equal zeta: the exponent sum is18, congruent to1 modulo17. Consequently the determinant image is exactly mu17, rather than a continuous circle. Finite local orders alone do not imply that the group generated by the loops is finite.

## Fixed-parameter irreducibility from bounded loops

The KZ module supplies the full Gaussian-rational loop matrices and ordinary complex-modulus matrix-infinity error bounds. The first loop is the rectangle about zero based at1/2. Crossing symmetry gives the second loop about one as V=XUX, with a separately propagated norm bound.

For each spectral root lambda_i form the exact polynomial spectral projector

\[
P_i(U)=\prod_{j\ne i}\frac{U-\lambda_j I}{\lambda_i-\lambda_j}.
\]

All roots are enclosed using rational alternating arctangent remainders in Machin's identity, rational real Taylor bounds for sine and cosine, and outward dyadic rounding. The branch is fixed by0<4atan(1/5)-atan(1/239)<1<pi/2. Scalar inverse balls are used only after their exclusion of zero has been certified. Matrix products propagate rigorous norm error. No decimal approximation to pi is a proof input.

The certificate tests every off-diagonal block P_j V P_i. All20 blocks are certified nonzero. The smallest center norm lower bound exceeds0.0547440, while the largest block error upper bound is below0.000002852. Every U-invariant subspace is a sum of its distinct one-dimensional eigenspaces. An invariant spectral subset must be closed under these directed blocks; the certified graph is strongly connected, so no proper subset is possible. Products of the blocks also yield all matrix units, proving that the actual loop algebra is M5(C).

This establishes irreducibility at hbar=1/34 itself. A residue Burnside calculation or a formal expansion near hbar=0 would not replace this specialization argument. Ball centers are rational-complex approximations to analytic holonomies; the code does not claim that those centers are the exact holonomies.

## Primitivity and infinitude

An irreducible imprimitive representation in dimension5 has a system of five lines. An order17 generator has trivial permutation in S5. Both U and V would therefore preserve every line, contradicting the established irreducibility. Gamma is primitive.

Rowell and Tuba, Lemma5.1(d), restrict the projective element orders in a finite primitive unimodular irreducible degree-five group to1 through6 and9 through12. The lemma applies to arbitrary such finite groups, not just braid images. A finite candidate Gamma can be replaced by its finite central extension in SL5 obtained by determinant-normalizing all elements, preserving primitivity and projective orders. Its projective order17 element violates that restriction. Hence Gamma is infinite. No explicit extension from pure loops to half-braids is required for this step.

## Unitarity and the connected component

The adjoint primary has affine level2. At level4 the fusion/tensor stability bound for theta tensor theta is attained: all five multiplicity-one channels of dimensions1,248,3875,30380,27000 survive, with affine levels0,2,2,3,4. Factorization therefore gives five four-adjoint singlet conformal blocks, equal to the full five-dimensional finite invariant fiber used by the connection. Belkale's genus-zero unitarity theorem applies. Dual conventions or scalar gauges preserve projective unitarity; determinant modulus one promotes a projectively preserved positive metric to a genuinely invariant one, since U*HU=cH implies c^5=|det U|^2=1 and c>0. The same holds for V. An explicit unitarizing matrix is not computed here.

Let K be the resulting compact closure and K0 its identity component. Clifford theory makes the K0-isotypic summands a transitive system under K. Prime dimension5 leaves three possibilities: an irreducible five-dimensional restriction, a system of five lines, or a scalar K0. Primitivity excludes the line system. A connected scalar subgroup with finite determinant is trivial; a compact Lie group with trivial identity component is finite, contradicting infinitude. Thus K0 acts irreducibly. Its Lie algebra is semisimple because its center acts scalarly and its determinant is trivial. Prime representation dimension also makes that Lie algebra simple.

The irreducible simple compact subalgebras in degree five are su5, so5 and su2 in its symmetric fourth power. The two proper candidates preserve a unique symmetric bilinear form. Since K normalizes K0, it would preserve that form up to scalar. U would then have a spectrum invariant under lambda -> c/lambda. Writing the exponent set as S={0,1,2,4,11}, such a symmetry requires S=a-S modulo17. Summing gives5a=2*sum(S)=2, hence a=14; but14-0 is absent from S. No such form is possible. Therefore K0=SU(5).

Finally, K contains SU(5) and its determinant image is exactly mu17, so it contains every SU(5) coset with those determinants and no others. This proves the displayed compact closure. Complexifying the connected component and retaining those determinant cosets proves the displayed Zariski closure.

The equation is regular singular at zero, one and infinity. Schlesinger
density therefore also identifies its pointwise differential Galois group
at this fixed level with `{g in GL(5,C):det(g)^17=1}`, with identity
component SL(5,C). This corollary uses the specialized loop theorem; it is
distinct from the formal and small infinite-order-parameter GL(5) statement
in [ASTERION_MONODROMY_ALGEBRA.md](ASTERION_MONODROMY_ALGEBRA.md).

Since5 and17 are coprime, the determinant sequence splits centrally: a determinant zeta^a is realized by the scalar zeta^(7a)I, since5*7=1 modulo17. The compact closure is therefore abstractly SU(5) times C17. This describes the closed group, rather than a presentation of its countable dense monodromy subgroup.

## Replay and boundary

`monodromy_receipt(exhaustive=True)` replays KZ normalization, the log-free local basis, bounded loop integration, root enclosures and the20 projector-block certificates. It retains the exact loop approximation matrices, all error bounds, and the theorem hypotheses. The computation takes roughly twenty seconds for the loops and less than one second for the projector graph on the reference environment. `monodromy_receipt(exhaustive=False)` records the structural gates and does not assert that a loop replay occurred.

`local_spectrum_receipt(level)` also reports exact spectral and determinant orders for other integer levels at least4. The group-closure theorem is currently specialized to level4. No other-level density theorem, explicit unitarizing matrix, presentation of the countable dense image, or proof of minimal arithmetic normalization is claimed.

## Primary sources

Eric C. Rowell and Imre Tuba, *Finite linear quotients of B3 of low dimension*, [arXiv:0806.0168](https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.0168), Lemma5.1(d), supplies the unconditional finite primitive degree-five obstruction.

Prakash Belkale, *Unitarity of the KZ/Hitchin connection on conformal blocks in genus0 for arbitrary Lie algebras*, [arXiv:1101.5846](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.5846), Theorem1.1 and Section10, supplies the preserved positive metric and explains projective descent.

Alex J. Feingold, *Fusion Rules for Affine Kac-Moody Algebras*, [arXiv:math/0212387](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0212387), Theorems6.1-6.2, supplies the fusion/tensor stability criterion.

Robert Zeier and Thomas Schulte-Herbrueggen, *Symmetry Principles in Quantum Systems Theory*, [arXiv:1012.5256](https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.5256), Table1 and the degree-five highest-weight table, supplies the compact irreducible subgroup list. The2014 erratum concerns a different table and leaves this degree-five list unchanged.
