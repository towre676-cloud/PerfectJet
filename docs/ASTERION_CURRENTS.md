# Asterion currents and exact Jordan-refined populations

The finite 248-dimensional E8 algebra is the mode-zero part of an infinite affine current algebra and the weight-one part of the level-one E8 lattice vertex algebra. The present implementation transports the actual Jordan bracket and normalized Killing form into those currents. It also computes exact universal and simple vacuum populations, refined by all 125 grades in the group A = F5^3. These are implementations and derived certificates for classical algebras and modular identities. They do not establish a new simple Lie algebra or a new geometric packing density.

Write J_(a,r)[m] for the current with nonzero Jordan grade a, fiber r in {0,1}, and integer mode m. The bracket is [J_(a,r)[m],J_(b,s)[n]] = sum_t beta_(ab,rs)^t J_(a+b,t)[m+n] + k m delta_(m+n,0) kappa_(ab,rs) K. Here beta is the executable 2A4/Fourier bracket, and kappa is the normalized Killing form, with (h_i,h_j)=A_ij and (e_alpha,e_-alpha)=1 in the Chevalley chart. The central identity K has the prescribed level k; the default is k=1. The degree derivation satisfies [d,J[m]]=m J[m] and [d,K]=0. At mode zero the central term vanishes, recovering the existing finite E8 exactly.

The invariant form pairs only opposite Jordan grades. In the actual basis it has exactly 248 nonzero ordered entries, one in each row, coupling 62 pairs of two-dimensional opposite-grade spaces. Its coefficients are integral in Z[zeta_5]. The implementation computes all 30,876 upper-triangular basis pairings through the rational Chevalley/2A4 transport. It then checks all 122,016 potentially nonzero ordered basis invariance identities; all other identities vanish by the independently verified grade orthogonality. Finite E8 Jacobi and invariant-form symmetry/invariance imply the affine Jacobi identity for every integer mode, rather than only a bounded mode panel. The finite Jordan Jacobi certificate is supplied by the separate exhaustive Jordan checker.

The index-five Cartan certificate is also the lattice gluing behind the vertex algebra. The E8 lattice is the union of five cosets of A4+A4, with gluing shifts (omega_i,omega_(2i)), i modulo five. The vacuum sector contributes the two sl5 adjoint algebras, dimension 48 at weight one. Each of the other four sectors contributes dimension 5 times 10, giving 48+4*50=248. This realizes the existing rational 2A4 model as an actual finite layer of an infinite lattice construction. The exact Cartan transport has determinant 1/5 in first-four-diagonal coordinates in each sl5 block, and integral inverse. This statement concerns the transported Chevalley lattice; the Jordan Fourier basis is not asserted to be unimodular over Z.

Let phi(q)=product_(n>=1)(1-q^n), with q=exp(2*pi*i*tau). The unshifted simple population series is Z(q)=E4(q)/phi(q)^8. The conformal character is q^(-1/3) Z(q)=E4(tau)/eta(tau)^8, with Sugawara central charge 248/(1+30)=8. The first simple dimensions are 1,248,4124,34752,213126,1057504,4530744,17333248. The universal affine vacuum module has PBW series U(q)=phi(q)^(-248), beginning 1,248,31124,2634752,169205626. It has the same level-one Sugawara central charge eight; the exponent 248 here is its PBW colored-partition growth parameter, not another assertion about that central charge.

The all-degree Jordan refinement follows from a conjugacy theorem, not from empirical equality of multiplicities. Any nonidentity element of the dual Jordan automorphism group has fixed finite Lie algebra dimension 24*2=48. Elduque's proof of Theorem 2.1, Step 2, identifies a unique conjugacy class of order-five E8 automorphisms with that fixed dimension. Thus all 124 nonidentity elements have the same trace on every conformal weight space. Lie automorphisms preserve the normalized form, extend to the universal affine vacuum algebra, and preserve its unique maximal ideal, hence descend to the simple level-one quotient. If T(q) is their common unshifted twining character, finite character orthogonality gives dim(V_n)_0=(Z_n+124*T_n)/125 and dim(V_n)_a=(Z_n-T_n)/125 for every nonzero a. The code verifies the exact cyclotomic Fourier Gram matrix via its 125 possible differences, certifying all 15,625 entries and the denominator-125 idempotent projectors.

The five lattice coset sectors give Z=A+4B and T=A-B, where A=Theta_A4(q)^2/phi(q)^8. Consequently T=(5*Theta_A4^2-E4)/(4*phi^8). The simplification T(q)=phi(q)^2/phi(q^5)^2 holds to all orders. For a finite proof, compare (5*Theta_A4^2-E4)/4 with eta(tau)^10/eta(5*tau)^2. Both are holomorphic weight-four modular forms for Gamma0(5), with trivial character. Theta_A4 has rank four, determinant five, level five, and quadratic character Legendre(d/5), so its square has trivial character. The eta exponents (10,-2) meet the two Newman congruences, with sums 0 and 48 modulo 24; its cusp orders are zero at infinity and two at zero. The group index is six, and the weight-four Sturm bound is floor(4*6/12)=2. Exact coefficients through q^2 are 1,-10,35 on both sides. Therefore the identity is all-order, with modularity as an explicit mathematical dependency. The implementation independently enumerates A4={(x_0,...,x_4) in Z^5:sum x_i=0} by norm/2 and replays further coefficients. Finite agreement beyond the Sturm bound is a regression check, not the premise replacing modularity.

For a nonidentity Jordan automorphism the adjoint eigenvalue multiplicities are 48,50,50,50,50. Applying PBW to each oscillator mode gives the universal twining series T_U=phi(q)^2/phi(q^5)^50. Hence universal populations and the relation space ker(V^1(E8) -> L_1(E8)) have the same exact two-class Fourier refinement. The relation twine is phi(q)^2*(phi(q^5)^(-50)-phi(q^5)^(-2)). It vanishes through degree four and begins 48*q^5. Thus the 27,000 quadratic relations are distributed uniformly, 216 in every one of the 125 grades. The degree-three relation population is likewise uniform, 20,800 per grade. At degree five the neutral relation population first exceeds each nonzero-grade population by exactly 48. This is a derived coefficient identity for the classical quotient; it is not claimed as an independent new classification theorem.

Neutral finite, loop, and vertex components must be kept separate. The finite Lie algebra has g_0=0. The current algebra has a neutral central generator and degree derivation. The vertex algebra has a neutral vacuum of dimension one, zero neutral states at conformal weight one, and 32 neutral states at weight two. The first neutral simple populations are 1,0,32,280,1706; the population in each nonzero grade begins 0,2,33,278,1705. Every higher-weight homogeneous component has its own growing multiplicity; the two-dimensional finite fiber rule applies only at weight one.

The retention density rho_n=dim L_1(E8)_n / dim V^1(E8)_n is an exact, basis-independent state-count ratio. The code also supplies neutral and nonzero-grade retention ratios and exact relation deficits. Its leading logarithmic asymptotics are log(dim L_1(E8)_n) ~ 4*pi*sqrt(n/3) and log(dim V^1(E8)_n) ~ 2*pi*sqrt(248*n/6), hence log rho_n ~ -33.140032389...*sqrt(n). These classical asymptotics do not provide a finite-degree error bound or a numerical runtime improvement.
