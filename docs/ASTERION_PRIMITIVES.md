# Explicit primitive generators of the E8 arc kernel through weight six

Post-v0.19.0 research report · 20 October 2026 · Exact mathematics

This chapter closes three gates left open by
[ASTERION_FRONTIERS.md](ASTERION_FRONTIERS.md): explicit highest representatives
with certified affine null lifts for the weight-five primitive modules, the
complete Li-bigraded embedding of the weight-six kernel, and the minimal list
of differential-ideal generators through weight six. It also decides the
individual associated-graded image of the invariant quartic F₀ and records the
first length-three sectors at weight seven. All arithmetic is exact. Worldwide
priority has not been established.

Notation is that of the earlier chapters: g=E8 in the package Chevalley basis,
V=L₁(E8), R=V/C₂(V)=Sym(g)/(I) with I=V(2θ), A=J∞R, and
K=ker(A→gr^Li V). Divided jets are x^[r]=Dʳx/r!. A monomial with current
length ℓ at conformal weight n has Li degree n−ℓ. We write x, y, z for the
universal zero, first and second jets (x=Σ tᵃx_a^[0], and so on), B for the
normalized form, u=e_θ and v=e_(θ−α₆).

## 1. An exact lattice criterion for null vectors

The vacuum module V¹(E8) has a unique maximal proper submodule N, and V=V¹/N.
The Frenkel–Kac construction identifies V with the lattice VOA V_L of the E8
root lattice. The module implements V_L exactly: a state is a sum of
oscillator monomials h_i(−n) times e^β, the root vertex operator is
Y(e^α,z)=E⁻(α,z)E⁺(α,z)e_α z^α(0), E⁺ acts by the translation
X_(n,j)↦X_(n,j)−(α,α_j)wⁿ, and E⁻ multiplies by Schur polynomials. Every mode
application is scaled by 8!, which clears all Schur denominators through
weight eight, so the arithmetic is in integers.

The package cocycle ε is bimultiplicative with ε(α,α)=(−1)^(|α|²/2); it is
the lattice cocycle. The map

\[
\varphi(h_i)=h_i(-1)|0\rangle,\qquad \varphi(e_\alpha)=\operatorname{sign}(\operatorname{ht}\alpha)\,e^\alpha
\]

satisfies φ(a)₍₀₎φ(b)=φ([a,b]), φ(a)₍₁₎φ(b)=B(a,b)|0⟩ and φ(a)₍ₘ₎φ(b)=0 for
m=2,3. All 61,504 ordered basis pairs are checked through the actual vertex
operators, not through a quoted structure-constant table. By Borcherds'
commutator formula the modes then satisfy the level-one affine relations, so
φ induces a module map V¹→V_L. Its image is generated by the weight-one
space, which is all of V_L, and V_L is simple. Hence its kernel is exactly N:

\[
v\in N\iff \varphi(v)=0 .
\]

This gives a certificate independent of contravariant Gram matrices. As a
first check, the retained 804-term weight-four lift
R_Q=Sym(F_Q)−8Q₃₁+24Q₂₂ maps to zero, while Sym(F_Q) alone has a 44-term
image. The number 44 is dim L₄^λ=p₈(2), as it must be.

## 2. Highest vectors, ideal highest vectors and nonmembership

For a target highest weight μ and conformal weight n, the length-three
polynomial space splits into jet shapes such as (0,0,2) and (0,1,1). Its
highest vectors are the kernel of the eight simple raising derivations. The
kernel is found by sparse elimination modulo the prime 2⁶¹−1, reconstructed
as rationals, and then verified exactly over Q. Each verified vector has a
distinct free pivot, so the verified family has at least the modular nullity.
That nullity bounds the rational nullity from above, so the family is a
complete rational basis. The modular step is never trusted by itself.

At length three and weight n the differential ideal is spanned by
z^[n−3−k]D^k(i), with z∈g, i∈I and 0≤k≤n−3. The map is equivariant. Its
highest vectors of weight μ are therefore the images of the highest vectors of
g⊗I. Those are cut out of g⊗Sym²g by (Ω−2)=0, the split-Casimir eigenvalue of
I; the other summands of Sym²g have −60 and −12. Complete reducibility makes
the following an exact test:

> a highest polynomial P is nonzero in A iff it is outside the span of the
> ideal's highest vectors of the same weight.

An independent second check uses the whole weight space. The ideal's weight-μ
component is spanned explicitly using an I-basis, each I_ν being ker(Ω−2) on
(Sym²g)_ν. The proved arc character fixes its rational rank as
dim(polynomials)_μ−dim A_μ. A modular rank equal to that value, rising by one
when P is adjoined, forces rational nonmembership, because modular rank never
exceeds rational rank.

Null lifts are searched on highest columns of every length at once. A null
vector whose leading Li symbol has length three has no length-four or
length-five terms, and every length component of a highest vector is itself
highest. The unknowns are therefore the highest columns of lengths three, two
and one. The lattice images of their PBW symmetrizations give a small exact
linear system. Its kernel is (N∩F^(n−3)) at that highest weight.

The A multiplicities agree with the independently proved weight-five
length-three character. Here dim L₅^μ is p₈(5−|μ|²/2), the length of the
lattice target, and the kernel multiplicities reproduce K₅=248+3875+147250.

**The 147250 primitive.** Let H₀₀₂ and H₀₁₁ be the primitive integral highest
vectors of weight ω₈ in the shapes (0,0,2) and (0,1,1); each has 56 terms.
Then

\[
P_{147250}=2H_{002}-3H_{011}
\]

has 112 terms with coefficients ±2, ±3. No length-two or length-one state has
weight ω₈, so Sym(P₁₄₇₂₅₀) is itself the null lift; its lattice image is
exactly zero. Since g⊗I has no ω₈ highest vector, P₁₄₇₂₅₀ is nonzero modulo
the ideal. The full-weight-space check confirms this: 168 columns, ideal
modular rank 159 (equal to the character prediction), and rank 160 after P is
adjoined.

**The 248 primitive.** The normalized representative is coordinate-free:

\[
\boxed{P_{248}=20\,u^{[0]}B(x,z)-21\,u^{[0]}B(y,y)-12\,u^{[1]}B(x,y)}
\]

with 615 monomials. Its null lift is

\[
\operatorname{Sym}(P_{248})-4K_{03}-87K_{12}+614\,u^{[4]},\qquad
K_{rs}=\sum_a[u,t_a]^{[r]}(t^a)^{[s]},
\]

with the lower terms also symmetrized, x^[r]↔x[−r−1]. Its lattice image
vanishes. The module checks both identities exactly against the searched
representative. Among the four null directions, three are the ideal's
highest vectors and one is new. The rank against the ideal's three highest
vectors rises from 3 to 4, and the full weight-space rank check also passes.
Thus u ↦ P₂₄₈(u) is an explicit equivariant copy of g in K₅, made only of the
Casimir contractions B(x,z), B(y,y), B(x,y).

**The 3875 class** is D F_Q: the derivative of the retained weight-four
symbol spans the same class modulo the ideal's highest vectors. This
recovers K₅'s translated part from the direct sector computation.

Since D K₄ is irreducible of type 3875 and g[0]K₄=0, neither 248 nor 147250 has
a highest vector in D K₄+g[0]K₄. The two representatives therefore generate
K₅/(D K₄+g[0]K₄)=V₂₄₈⊕V₁₄₇₂₅₀ explicitly.

## 4. Weight six

### Length three, Li degree three

| Highest weight | A multiplicity | Ideal rank | Null dimension | Kernel multiplicity | Kernel class |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 30380 (ω₆) | 3 | 4 | 5 | 1 | new primitive P₃₀₃₈₀ |
| 147250 | 3 | 0 | 1 | 1 | D P₁₄₇₂₅₀ |
| 3875 | 4 | 0 | 1 | 1 | D² F_Q |
| 248 | 5 | 4 | derived | 1 (forced) | D P₂₄₈ |
| 1 | 1 | 0 | not computed | 0 (forced) | none |

The 248 row is certified through the derivative rather than a column search.
D=L₋₁ preserves N, so Sym(D P₂₄₈)+Sym(D·correction) is an explicit null
lift. Its lattice image is checked to vanish: 1369 symbol terms and 145
correction terms. D is injective on positive ordinary degree, and the
highest-vector rank against the ideal's four highest vectors rises to five.
The multiplicity is forced: K₆ is exact, its length-at-most-two part vanishes,
and the length-four part (below) contains no 248.

The singlet row is forced rather than computed. K₆ has exactly one singlet,
and the length-four singlet below is a product in K. A direct lattice run on
the 32,920-monomial invariant column would be redundant.

The forced V₃₀₃₈₀ primitive has a pure length-three null lift. Its
PBW symmetrization alone has zero lattice image. Its 825 monomials have the
exact closed form

\[
\boxed{P_{30380}=6A_{003}+2A_{012}+12A_{021}+10A_{102}+2B_{012}-\tfrac56B_{111}}
\]

with

\[
A_{rst}=\sum_a\bigl([u,t_a]^{(r)}v^{(s)}-[v,t_a]^{(r)}u^{(s)}\bigr)(t^a)^{(t)},\qquad
B_{rst}=\sum_{a,b}[[u,t_a],[v,t_b]]^{(r)}(t^a)^{(s)}(t^b)^{(t)}-(u\leftrightarrow v).
\]

The 14 contraction tensors of these two types span the seven-dimensional
highest space. An exhaustive search shows that no combination of five or
fewer of them equals P₃₀₃₈₀. The ideal's highest-vector rank rises from 4 to 5,
and the full weight-space check passes. D K₅ and D²K₄ contain no 30380, and
every product of lower kernel classes has length at least four. P₃₀₃₈₀ is
therefore a new differential generator.

### Length four, Li degree two

The retained quartic theorem gives A₆^(len 4)≅1⊕3875, dual to
ker[μ:Sym²W→Sym⁴g]. Both summands are products of lower kernel classes.

*The 3875.* The dual space of weight −λ is computed directly in
Sym²W∩ker μ. W_ν=ker((Ω+12)(Ω+60)) on each weight space, and there are 967
pair columns. It is exactly one-dimensional. By Schur's lemma,
u⊗f↦x_u^[1]f followed by projection to the 3875 summand is a multiple of the
action u·f. The functional takes the value 63 on x_(h₀)^[1]F_Q and 0 on the
other seven Cartan directions, proportional to λ(h_i)=(1,0,…,0). The syzygy
x^[0]·DF_Q=−x^[1]F_Q in A, which comes from differentiating x^[0]F_Q=0, is
replayed as the value −63. Hence g[1]·K₄ fills the length-four 3875.

*The singlet.* The invariant functional T₀=2Id−Ω−2|C⟩⟨C| of the quartic
chapter is checked to annihilate the whole length-four ideal. By invariance it
suffices to test e_θ² against every complementary monomial for I_x·Sym²_y,
Sym²_x·I_y and the crossed relations i(x,y)q(x,y): 123,256 evaluations. Put

\[
Y=\sum_a x_{(t^a)}^{[0]}\,P_{248}^{(t_a)},
\]

the x^[0]-contraction of the weight-five 248 module. Then (u,v)↦T₀(x_uP^(v))
is an invariant form, κB, so T₀(Y)=248κ with κ=T₀(x_(−θ)P₂₄₈)=6510. This is
nonzero. The singlet of A₆^(len 4) is one-dimensional and T₀(F₀)=⟨T₀,T₀⟩=947856,
which the module rederives from the Ω spectrum. Therefore

\[
\boxed{F_0\equiv c\,Y \pmod{\text{arc ideal}},\qquad c=\frac{947856}{248\kappa}=\frac{91}{155}.}
\]

F₀ is a nonzero kernel class with zero image in gr^Li V. It is the
x^[0]-contraction of the weight-five 248 primitive, not a new relation.

### The Li-bigraded kernel

\[
K_6=\underbrace{(V_{248}\oplus V_{3875}\oplus V_{30380}\oplus V_{147250})}_{\text{Li degree 3, length 3}}
\;\oplus\;
\underbrace{(\mathbf 1\oplus V_{3875})}_{\text{Li degree 2, length 4}},
\]

and the Li-degree-four and Li-degree-five pieces vanish. The retained affine
Gram blocks give injectivity in length at most two. The length-four piece is
all of A₆^(len 4) by the two product functionals. The exact total K₆
character then forces the length-three piece. The direct sector computations
confirm its 3875, 30380 and 147250 multiplicities (each one).

## 7. Replay, evidence and boundaries

```sh
PYTHONPATH=src python -m perfectjet asterion primitives            # fast: stored certificates replayed
PYTHONPATH=src python -m perfectjet asterion primitives --exhaustive  # repeats the searches and lattice replays
PYTHONPATH=src python scripts/build_asterion_primitives.py          # regenerates stored representatives
PYTHONPATH=src python -m unittest tests.test_asterion_primitives -v
```

`primitives_receipt` returns the lattice bracket certificate and the R_Q
replay. It also returns every sector table and representative, both
nonmembership certificates, the derivative identifications, the closed-form
identities, the two length-four decisions, the Li-bigraded table and the
generator list. Its default mode searches the four fast sectors and verifies
the stored θ and weight-six representatives without the expensive lattice
replay. The exhaustive mode repeats every column search, compares SHA256
digests with the stored file, and replays every null lift in V_L, including
the derivative lift of the weight-six 248. It also recomputes the listed
weight-seven sectors. Tampered coefficients,
dropped corrections and stale digests are rejected by the tests.

The claims are finite: they cover conformal weights at most six, plus the
listed weight-seven sectors. They concern the commutative differential ideal
of the arc algebra, not strong generators of an unfiltered vertex algebra.
They do not assert finite generation of K, a vertex-Poisson presentation or
stabilization of the generator pattern. The Frenkel–Kac identification and
Borcherds' commutator formula are classical inputs; the lattice computation
checks every bracket and form value it depends on.
