# Asterion research programme

This programme preserves 64 mathematical directions with stable identifiers. Each entry states a concrete acceptance gate. Completed finite seeds and finite theorems are distinguished from their larger extensions; no entry asserts worldwide priority. Priority 1 marks a short dependency path to a checkable result, not absolute mathematical importance. The discovery ledger retains exact assertions and evidence paths.
